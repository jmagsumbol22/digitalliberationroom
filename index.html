Digital Liberation Room

Welcome to the Digital Liberation Room, U.S.A. You have been brought here because it is your 18th birthday and you're about to head to an expensive college, but someone sees a light within you that must not be extinguished. Right now, what you are witnessing is the system you were born into, connected by binaries, ones and zeros, trying to mold and control you, wrap you so tightly by the bureaucracy of the educational system that you won't be able to move. But I am going to help you find your way out. Take the red pill, and after completing three successful simulations, you will be the narrator of your own story. Take the blue pill, and you will continue to be led, comfortably, but unconsciously, like a sheep, to the slaughter of your spirit.

Digital Liberation Room

Digital Liberation Room: Challenge 1

You are the child of a single mother. Your mother had very little options in life and you have worked at the corner store since you were fourteen, saving money to go to college because you have been told since birth that college was the only sure way to be successful, to have agency, independence, and freedom in America. From your mother, to your church, to the ads on television, anyone can make it in America as long as they have a degree. You worked tirelessly for a near perfect GPA, exceptional SAT and ACT scores, and had a meaningful impact as a volunteer at the local food bank. You've made into an incredible four-year university in the big city, but at the end of high school, you only have $2,000 to your name and the tuition is $60,000 per year. What do you do to make it in America?

Digital Liberation Room: Challenge 1 — FAILED

You can NOT, make it in America.

Digital Liberation Room: Challenge 2

Congratulations, you took the financial aid package and are now at university. You're well on your way to making it in America. To cover your tuition, based on merit and need you were awarded $10k in scholarship funds, $10k in grants, $10k in work study, $20k in federal unsubsidized loans at 2% interest and $10k in federal subsidized loans at 2% interest — totaling $60,000. To cover your housing fees, $2k/month for ten months, you took out a personal loan at 8% interest. You have to spend money to make money. The key to freedom is education. You've wanted to be a painter all your life. Now, what are you going to study, that will make you feel like you have made it in America?

Digital Liberation Room: Challenge 2 — FAILED

You can NOT, make it in America.

Digital Liberation Room: Challenge 3

Majoring in finance sure is the wise move. The only thing is, you had to move off campus because the $2k a month housing fee was too steep. An hour and a half was added to your commute five days a week on top of the 30 hours a week you had to work to keep your work-study. You spent your remainder at school burning the candle at both ends, but that's the hustle! Somehow you were able to keep a steady GPA and were offered an internship at a financial firm. The internship is unpaid. What do you do to make it in America?

Digital Liberation Room: Conclusion

Poor thing, you took the internship but were late multiple times and weren't exhibiting "proper" hygiene — the firm had to let you go. Luckily, you'd only missed rent once because you were fired quickly enough to go back to your work study and graduate. But with burned bridges from the internship and accruing interest on your student loans, you need to take any job you can find immediately. You were smart to compromise your dream of painting and get that finance degree, but for now, just to survive, you'll have to compromise once more and take whatever is available. Maybe you will be able to make it in America, but not right now.
